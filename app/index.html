Eagles PDF viewer

Open a folder to see every PDF inside it, including subfolders.

  • Finger — drag to pan, pinch to zoom, double-tap to zoom
  • Pen — writes with the selected tool; flip it over to erase
  • Mouse — drag over text to copy it, middle-drag to pan
  • Signature — add a photo of it once; the background is removed for you
  • Ctrl+S saves annotations straight into the PDF
