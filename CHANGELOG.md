# Changes

The first lines under each version are shown in the app's update window.

## 1.12.0

- Tabs: opening a PDF adds a tab, so the one you were working on stays open. Tap a tab (or Ctrl+Tab) to switch; each keeps its page, zoom, undo and unsaved changes.
- Close a tab with its ×, Ctrl+W or a middle-click; drag tabs to reorder them (with a finger: hold a moment, or pull the tab down); right-click or hold a tab for its menu.
- Side by side, each pane has its own tabs: drag a tab onto another pane to move it there, or to a pane's edge for a new pane on that side.
- Tabs out of view sleep to save memory, and the same tabs open again when you start the app.

## 1.11.1

- Updates install again: after Restart now, the app closes only once its installer helper is running, and reopens on the new version. (1.10.x downloaded updates but couldn't install them; install this version once with the installer.)
- If an update ever can't install itself, the next start says so, shows what happened and offers the installer; after one that worked, a message says so.
- The tick box in the update window is the right size again.

## 1.11.0

- The Pages panel: make the pages bigger or smaller with the slider at its top, a two-finger pinch or Ctrl + mouse wheel.
- Drag the panel's left edge to make it wider or narrower (double-click for normal width); small pages or a wide panel show a grid of pages, which you can drag around too.
- The grip for moving pages with a finger is smaller, and still easy to hit.

## 1.10.2

- Updates now come from the app's own GitHub page, github.com/RukasuRi-96/PDF-windowsSurfaceViewer, which is public; the download link there works without signing in.
- Updates download the installer and take the app out of it, so each version is a single file.

## 1.10.1

- Updates came from a separate public page (since replaced by the app's own GitHub page in 1.10.2).
- The update window lists what changed in every version since yours.

## 1.10.0

- Automatic updates: the app tells you when a new version is out and installs it for you (⋯ → Check for updates).
- The download link now works without signing in to GitHub.

## 1.9.1

- The lasso drawn with the pen's side button now appears right under the pen on high-resolution screens such as the Surface Pro.

## 1.9.0

- Lasso select: hold the pen's side button (or choose Lasso) and draw round markups to move, resize, copy, paste, duplicate or delete them together.
- Search in a PDF (Ctrl+F), with every match marked and a list of results.
- A list of every markup in the PDF, with filters.
- Autosave, which you can turn on or off.
- Choose what the toolbar shows; its buttons shrink to fit, so nothing overlaps.
- The highlighter can highlight words or draw freely.
- The app's icon at the top left.

## 1.8.0

- Every PDF opened side by side can be written on; up to four, each with its own title bar to move or close it.
- Paste without a keyboard: tap with the Snip tool, or press and hold the page.
- Drag pages from the Pages panel onto another open PDF to copy them; a grip moves pages with a finger.

## 1.7.0

- Open a second PDF side by side.
- Snip: copy part of a page as a picture and paste it at the same size.
- Page names (labels and bookmarks) in the Pages panel and the page counter.
- Press and hold a page in the Pages panel to move it.

## 1.6.0

- Merge PDFs, or every PDF in a folder.
- The Pages panel: delete, add, copy in, duplicate, move and rotate pages.
